# Family chat

Chat gives each enabled family a shared group for quick messages that do not need to become a feed post. You can also start private one-to-one conversations with people who share a family with you.

Open **Chat** from the app navigation. The inbox shows both family chats and any private conversations you already started. Family chats are always visible - just select one to start talking to your family. Create private conversations by clicking on the "New conversation" button on the top-right of the inbox.

Once inside a chat, you can send text or attach a photo or video, and reply to a particular message to keep a conversation in context. New messages appear automatically while chat is open; an unread badge helps you spot activity when you are elsewhere in the app.

Chat is available in the web and mobile apps when the family administrator has enabled it for that family. Only members of that family can access its chat.
