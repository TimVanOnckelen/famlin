# Chat

Chat is the quick-conversation area of Famlin. It holds two different kinds of conversation:

- **Direct messages** are private, one-to-one conversations.
- **Family chats** are shared conversations inside one family, when that family's administrator has enabled chat.

Open **Chat** from the app navigation. The inbox separates direct messages from family chats; on mobile, **Search chats** filters both lists by conversation name. Choose **New message**, then search for the person you want to contact. You can only choose someone with whom you currently share at least one family. The conversation does not move into either family, so it can continue only while that shared-family relationship exists.

Both conversation types support text, a photo or video attachment, and replies to a particular message. New messages and unread counts refresh while Chat is active, and the Chat badge is checked periodically elsewhere in the app.

You can permanently delete a message you sent. In a family chat an administrator can also delete messages for moderation; but direct messages cannot be moderated or removed by admins.

## Privacy follows family membership

Sharing a family with someone is a live requirement: if either person stops belonging to the last family you share, the direct conversation and its attachments become unavailable to both of you. Access returns if you share a family again.

Family-chat and direct-message pushes use the same **Chat messages** switch in **Profile → Notifications**. Direct-message copy is deliberately generic — it identifies a direct message without putting the message text in a lock-screen notification.

## Encrypted private messages

If your server's administrator has turned on encryption, private one-to-one messages sent from the mobile app are end-to-end encrypted: only you and the other person can read them, not the server or its administrator. You don't need to set anything up — the app creates your keys the first time you open a private conversation.

Encryption is off by default. While it is off, every private conversation tells you that the server's administrator, and anyone with access to the server or its backups, can read your messages.

:::warning Lost keys mean lost messages

Nobody can recover encrypted messages for you — not your administrator, and not from a server backup. If you lose your phone without a keychain backup or a recovery code (see [New phone](#new-phone)), your encrypted messages are gone for good.

:::

- A small lock under a message means it is encrypted. **Not encrypted** means it was sent without encryption, for example from the web app (which can't encrypt), or before the other person had opened a private conversation in the app. A banner above the message box tells you whether your next message will be encrypted, and why not if it won't be.
- Either person can turn encryption off for a conversation from the conversation menu. Both of you then see a note in the conversation, and you can turn it back on the same way.
- The web app shows encrypted messages as a lock with a note to open the conversation in the app.
- Encrypted conversations support text and photos; videos can't be sent in them yet.

### New phone

Your keys are saved automatically in your phone's own keychain backup: iCloud Keychain on iPhone, your Google backup on Android. On a new phone signed in to the same Apple or Google account (and on Android, restored from that backup), your old messages are readable again without doing anything.

Moving between an iPhone and an Android phone, or with that backup turned off, you have two other options:

- **Scan from your old phone.** On the new phone, choose **Restore your encrypted messages → Scan from my other phone**, then on the old phone go to **Profile → Encrypted messages → Link a new device** and scan the code.
- **Recovery code.** Under **Profile → Encrypted messages** you can create a recovery code for extra safety. Keep it somewhere safe, such as a password manager. Nobody, including your administrator, can recover it for you.

If none of these work, choose **Start over**: new messages work right away, and the other person still has your whole conversation.

To check that nobody has swapped the keys, compare the **safety code** with the other person in person (from the conversation menu), or scan each other's code.

## Current limits

Direct messages are currently one-to-one; there are no group DMs or edits. Famlin stores both kinds of chat on your server. Member exports include only conversations they can currently access; server backups include the stored chat data and attachments. Family chats, and direct messages sent without encryption, are stored readable on the server; see [Encrypted private messages](#encrypted-private-messages). A connected server that does not offer direct messages keeps the original family-chat experience.
