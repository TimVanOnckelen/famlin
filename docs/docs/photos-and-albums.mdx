# Photos and albums

Famlin keeps shared photos close to the family updates they came from, and also offers a dedicated place to browse them together.

## Browse the Photos view

Open **Photos** to browse family photos and videos in a timeline grouped by month. Choose a family and, where available, filter by a person to narrow the timeline. Tap an item to open it; videos can be played from the viewer.

Photos added to posts remain connected to those posts, so you can return to the update and its conversation. The photo timeline and family filters are available in the mobile and web apps.

## Make a collaborative album

Choose **Album** when creating a post, give the album a title, and optionally add a description and starting photos. It appears in the family's feed and Photos view. Open the album to browse its images, see who contributed, and join the discussion.

Any member of the family can add photos while an album is open. The person who created it can close it when contributions are finished; a closed album remains viewable but no longer accepts new photos. Albums work in the web and mobile apps.

## Start a photo post

To share a few photos without starting an album, create an **Update** or **Milestone** and attach up to 10 photos or videos. On a phone, you can also share photos into Famlin from your device's photo app using **Share → Famlin**. See [Feed and updates](./sharing-updates.mdx) for post formats and attachment limits.
