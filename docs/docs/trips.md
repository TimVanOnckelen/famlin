# Trips

A **Trip** is a travel journal your family can follow through check-ins, photos
and updates. You can start a trip in either the mobile app or the web app.

## Start a trip on the web

1. Create a post, choose the families to share it with, and select **Trip**.
2. Enter a title and start date. A destination and end date are optional.
   The end date cannot be before the start date; moving the start past an
   existing end date clears the end date.
3. Optionally choose a cover photo and travel companions. The author is always
   a traveler. You can choose up to 20 additional companions, each of whom must
   belong to every family selected for this trip.
4. Click **Post**. If you chose a cover, it uploads before the trip is created.
   An upload failure keeps the form open so you can retry.

Trips can be shared with multiple families, provided they all allow Trip posts.
For one family, you can narrow the audience to a circle you belong to. Changing
families removes any selected companions who are no longer eligible.

## Follow the journey

Open a trip from the feed to see its check-ins, photos and travel companions.
The author and travel companions can add check-ins on either the web or the
mobile app, once the trip has started and until it is closed.

## Add a check-in on the web

1. Open the trip and click **Check-in**. Before the first check-in, this button
   appears in the empty timeline; afterwards it appears above the timeline.
2. Enter the place (required, up to 80 characters). Optionally add a note
   (up to 2,000 characters) and up to 10 photos or videos.
3. Remove any unwanted attachment with its × button, then click **Check in**.

Attachments upload before the check-in is saved. While saving, the form cannot
be dismissed or submitted again. If uploading or saving fails, the form keeps
its values so you can retry. A retry after a save error reuses files that
already uploaded successfully. After saving, the timeline and trip statistics
refresh automatically, along with the trip card in the feed.

Other family members can view, react and reply to check-ins, but cannot add
one unless they are a travel companion. Closed trips and trips that have not
started do not offer the check-in action. These rules are also enforced by the
server.

Use the mobile app to close a trip or change its travel companions later.
