# Search and favorites

## Search a family's posts

Use **Search** to look for a post in one of your families. Choose the family, enter a word or phrase, then open a result to return to the post. Search is available in both the web and mobile apps and only returns content you are allowed to see in that family. In the web app, press `/` (or `g` then `s`) to jump to search from anywhere.

## Save a post for later

Tap or click the bookmark icon on a post to add it to **Favorites**. Open Favorites from the app navigation to find your saved posts again. Saving a post is personal to your account; it does not change who can see the original post.

Both search and favorites follow Famlin's family visibility rules. A gift list is only visible to its participants, so it does not appear to other family members in search or favorites.
