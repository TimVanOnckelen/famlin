# Using Famlin

Famlin is a private place for the people in your family groups to share everyday updates, photos, plans, and conversations. Use the iOS or Android app, or sign in to your Famlin server in a browser.

## Find your way around

| Feature | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [Feed and updates](./sharing-updates) | Follow family posts, share photos and videos, celebrate milestones, ask questions with polls, and revisit memories from this day in past years. |
| [Stories](./stories) | Share a photo moment for 24 hours, or keep it in Highlights. Create stories in the mobile app; view them on mobile or web. |
| [Photos and albums](./photos-and-albums) | Browse a family photo timeline or build a shared album together. |
| [Chat](./chat) | Talk with members in a family's shared chat. |
| [Search and favorites](./search-and-favorites) | Find a post in a family or bookmark one to come back to. |
| [Notifications and profile](./notifications-and-profile) | Keep up with activity, update your profile, choose preferences, or export your data. |
| [Trips](./trips) | Collect travel check-ins, photos, and companions in a journey timeline. |
| [Gift lists](./gift-lists) | Share wishlists for an occasion and optionally run a private Secret Santa draw. |

## Families and privacy

Your feed can show posts from all your families or be filtered to one family. When you share, choose the family or families that should see the post. If you belong to a **circle** in a family, you can narrow a post's audience to that circle. Circle sharing is for one family at a time; it can't be combined with sharing to multiple families.

Famlin's server also lets its administrator choose which post formats, stories, chat, and integrations are available in each family. If something in this guide is missing from your app, ask your family administrator whether it is enabled. For server setup and administration, see [Self-hosting](./server-setup).

## App and browser

Most everyday features are available in both the mobile apps and the web app. Stories are the exception: you can create them in the mobile app and view them in either app. Some screens and controls differ slightly by device; the linked guides call out important differences.
