# Stories

Stories are photo moments that stay in the live story tray for **24 hours**. If the author pins a story to **Highlights**, it remains available after that. Stories appear near the top of the feed for families where the feature is enabled.

## Create a story

Story creation is available in the iOS and Android apps:

1. Open the feed and tap the **+** on your story bubble.
2. Take a photo or choose one from your photo library.
3. Add text, emoji stickers, or freehand drawing if you like.
4. Choose the family or families who can see it. For one family, you can choose a circle you belong to.
5. Share the story.

Stories are photo-based; video selection is not available in the story composer. The web app can display stories and Highlights, but story creation is mobile-only.

## View and respond

Tap a family member's story bubble to view their stories in sequence. The viewer advances automatically; tap to move between stories. A colored ring marks stories you have not viewed yet. Pinned stories are collected in the **Highlights** row.

Viewers can react or send one reply to the story's author. Story replies are private to the author and the person who sent them—they do not appear as public feed comments. Authors can see who viewed, reacted, or replied, and can pin or delete their stories.

## If stories are missing

Stories only appear for families where the administrator has turned them on. They also follow the family filter selected in your feed. Ask your family administrator if you think stories should be available but cannot see them.
