# Notifications and profile

## Keep up with activity

Open **Notifications** to review activity such as new posts, comments, reactions, stories, and gift-list updates. You can mark notifications as read. Depending on what your server administrator has configured, Famlin may also send push notifications or email. If your administrator has set the server's [public address](./admin-configuration.md), notification emails include a link straight to the post in the web app.

Choose which activity you want to hear about in **Profile → Notifications**. Push and email preferences are separate where both delivery methods are available. Story notifications are push-only.

## Update your profile

From **Profile**, you can change your profile photo, choose the app's display language, review your gift lists, and manage account settings. Famlin's interface is available in English, Dutch, and Simplified Chinese.

On web, the **Personal details** section lets you edit your name and email address. Select **Save details** to apply both changes. The email must be valid and cannot already belong to another account; if it is taken, neither field is changed. For password sign-in, use the new email address next time you log in.

If your account uses password sign-in, you can change your password from your profile. You can also request a downloadable export of your Famlin data or delete your account. Account deletion is permanent; review the confirmation carefully before proceeding.

In the web app, the **Connect your phone** section of your profile shows a QR code for this server. Scan it with your phone's camera to open the Famlin app pointed at the right server; see [Connecting the mobile app with a QR code](./inviting-family.md#connecting-the-mobile-app-with-a-qr-code).

## Family members and server administration

Open a family's member list to see who belongs to it. In the web app, **Members** in your avatar menu (or the `g` then `m` keyboard shortcut) opens a page listing every family you belong to, plus the circles you're in. Inviting someone new and configuring groups, enabled features, notifications, or storage are administrator tasks; see [Inviting family members](./inviting-family.md) and [Admin configuration](./admin-configuration.md).

## Crop your profile photo on web

Open your profile and select your photo to choose a new image. Drag it inside
the full-width preview to position it and use the **+** and **−** buttons in
the lower-right corner to zoom, then
select **Save**. The circular preview shows how the square crop appears as an
avatar. **Cancel** leaves your current photo unchanged. Browser-readable images are uploaded only after you save.

The web app crops and compresses the image on your device before uploading:
JPEG at 80% quality, up to 512 × 512 pixels. Smaller crops are not enlarged.
If your browser cannot display HEIC/HEIF, the original is first uploaded for server-side conversion. Wait for processing to finish, then crop the converted JPEG and save as usual. Cancelling stops waiting for conversion and does not change your profile photo; the original may already have reached the server. Other unsupported or damaged images show an error. Existing profile photos are unchanged.
