# Feed and updates

The feed is the shared timeline for your families. Use the family filters at the top to see everything together or focus on one family. On a post, you can react, comment, and save it to your favorites. The post's audience is shown with it.

## Choose what to share

Tap **New post** (the plus button in the mobile app, or the new-post button on the web) and choose a format. The family administrator controls which formats are available in each family.

- **Update** — write a note for your family and optionally attach photos or videos. In the mobile app, you can also add a place.
- **Milestone** — mark a birthday, birth, anniversary, graduation, or another special moment.
- **Poll** — ask a question and add 2–10 answer choices. Family members can vote and see the results.
- **Event** — invite the family to a get-together with a start time and, optionally, an end time, a place, and a reply-by date. Everyone answers **Going**, **Maybe**, or **Can't go** and can see who's coming. Replies close at the reply-by date, or when the event starts if you didn't set one. Answer the same way again to take your reply back. An event can be shared with several families at once: each family sees its own members' replies. Events are available in the web app; until the mobile app supports them, they show there as a plain post with the event's title.
- **Trip** — start a travel journal with dates, destination, cover photo, and travel companions. See [Trips](./trips.md).
- **Album** — start a shared photo album that family members can add to. See [Photos and albums](./photos-and-albums.mdx).
- **Gift list** — collect wishes for an occasion and optionally draw Secret Santa names. See [Gift lists](./gift-lists.md).

## Add photos, videos, or a place

Updates and milestones can include photos and videos—up to 10 attachments per post. Videos can be up to 2 minutes long. In the mobile app, you can also add a place to an update when it helps tell the story.

### Share from another app

Looking at a photo in Photos, Google Photos, Files, WhatsApp or another app? Tap **Share → Famlin** to open a post with the selected photos or videos attached. Choose the audience, add a caption, and share as usual. On iPhone, Famlin appears in the share sheet as **Famlin Share**. If you don't see it, scroll the app row to the end, tap **More**, and switch it on.

- Shared photos and videos go into one post. A post holds at most **10** attachments; anything beyond that is skipped, and the app tells you how many.
- Only photos and videos can be shared—not documents or links. Videos can be up to **2 minutes** long, the same as when you pick media inside Famlin.
- If you aren't signed in, log in first; Famlin then opens the post with your shared media still attached.
- If you're already writing a post, the shared photos and videos are added to it.
- Shared media goes to the server you're currently signed in to.

## Revisit a memory

When you filter the feed to a single family, Famlin can surface posts that were shared on the same date in earlier years under **On this day**. Open the memory to revisit the original post and its conversation.

## Choose who can see a post

Select one or more families before publishing. A post shared with multiple families appears in each selected family's feed. If you choose just one family and belong to one of its circles, you can narrow the audience to that circle. A circle audience and multi-family sharing can't be combined.

Gift lists are always for one family and are visible only to their participants. Trips can be shared with multiple families when each family allows trip posts. The app only offers formats that all selected families allow.
