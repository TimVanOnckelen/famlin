The Family Wiki: durable, editable family knowledge (recipes, the
holiday house's Wi-Fi, traditions), one wiki per family (group). Any
member who can see a page can edit it, unless the page is `locked`
(then only its creator or an admin). A page can be narrowed to one
circle at creation; circle-private pages are invisible to everyone
outside the circle (`404`, never `403`). `content` is a small, safe
Markdown subset: headings, bold/italic, lists, checklists, quotes, code,
rules, links to `http(s)`/`mailto:`/`tel:` or in-app paths such as
`/posts/<id>`, and images from uploaded `/uploads/` files only.
Concurrent edits are detected with `baseUpdatedAt`.
