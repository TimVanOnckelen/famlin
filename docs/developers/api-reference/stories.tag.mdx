Photos shared for 24 hours, Instagram-style. Same audience rules as posts
(one group, several, or one circle) plus the per-group `storiesEnabled`
toggle. Viewers can react and send one private reply; only the author
sees who viewed, reacted and replied. An author can pin a live story to
keep it forever as one of the group's Highlights; an unpinned story is
permanently deleted — photo included — once its 24 hours are up.
