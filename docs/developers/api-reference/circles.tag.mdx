Smaller, reusable audiences inside one family group ("Grandparents",
"Cousins"). A circle narrows group membership, it never widens it:
every circle member is already a member of the group behind it.

Circles are **fully private**. Only the circles you belong to are ever
returned to you — a group member outside a circle cannot see its name,
its members, or the posts in it, and has no way to learn it exists.
Creating, editing and deleting circles, and managing their membership,
are admin operations and are not part of this member-facing API; the
one mutation you can perform is leaving a circle you're in.
