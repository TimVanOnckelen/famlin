Family chats and private one-to-one direct messages. Family chat remains
the original per-group feature (off by default, toggled by admins), and
its legacy routes and response schemas stay available unchanged. Servers
with the `directMessages` feature also expose generalized conversation
routes for a combined inbox, direct-message creation, message history,
unread counts, and read positions.

A private conversation is visible only to its two participants while they
currently share at least one family. That is a live authorization check,
not a grant remembered when the conversation was created; losing the
shared-family relationship hides the conversation and its uploads.
Private conversation/message routes return `404` when the conversation is
unknown or inaccessible, and server admins receive no special bypass.
