Who can help with what

Famlin is self-hosted: the app connects to a server that someone in your family runs, not to a service operated by the Famlin project. That splits support in two.

  • Your family's admin — whoever set up your server. Ask them about invites, being added to a family, missing posts, someone else's account, or getting your data removed from their server.
  • The Famlin project (the links above) — the app itself: crashes, bugs, something that looks broken on every server, questions about self-hosting, or feature ideas.

Common questions

The app asks for a server address. What do I enter?

Famlin has no central sign-up — you need the web address of your family's own Famlin server (something like https://famlin.yourfamily.example). Whoever invited you has it; it's the same address as the invite link they sent. If nobody in your family runs a server yet, one person needs to set one up first — see Self-hosting Famlin.

How do I join my family?

Your admin sends you an invite link. Opening it on a phone with the app installed takes you straight into the join screen; opening it in a browser shows the same invite with a download link. Invites expire, so ask for a fresh one if yours has run out. More in Inviting your family.

How do I sign in?

Depending on how your admin configured the server, you can sign in with an email and password, with Sign in with Apple, or with the single-sign-on provider they set up. All available options appear on the login screen once you've entered the server address.

I forgot my password.

Ask your family's admin to reset it from the Famlin admin panel. Because each server is independent, the Famlin project can't reset a password for you.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Delete account → Delete my account, then type DELETE to confirm. This is immediate and permanent: your profile, posts, photos, comments, reactions and chat messages are removed from the server, and there is no way to restore them. The same option is in the web app's profile page. If you're the only admin on your server, make someone else an admin first — otherwise nobody could manage it after you leave.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check Profile → Notifications in the app, and that notifications are allowed for Famlin in your device's system settings. Your admin can also turn push notifications off for the whole server, in which case the toggles are hidden.

Photos won't upload, or the app can't reach the server.

This is almost always the server rather than the app: check whether other family members are affected too, and ask your admin. If it's only you, sign out and back in — and make sure the server address you entered still matches the one they gave you.

Where does my data live, and who can see it?

Everything stays on your family's own server. There's no analytics or tracking in the app, and nothing is sent back to the project. See the privacy policy for the details.
