A private place for your family, on a server you own.

Famlin is an open-source family app you host yourself. Share photos, milestones, trips and the small everyday stuff with the people who actually care, in plain chronological order. No ads, no recommendations, no company in between.

What your family gets

One app for several families. Grandma can follow both sides of the family in one feed, and still see which family each post came from.

A shared feed

Post text, photos and videos to one family or several at once. Everyone can react, comment (with photos of their own) and @mention someone so they get a notification. Big moments like a birthday or a first day of school can be posted as a milestone, so they stand out.

Save posts to your favorites, search older posts, and get an "On this day" reminder of what you shared in earlier years.

How the feed works →

Trips, written together

Start a trip with a destination, dates and the people coming along. While you travel, everyone on the trip can check in from each stop with a place name, a note and photos. Back home, the family sees one journal with a day counter and a timeline instead of thirty loose posts.

How trips work →

Polls and gift lists

Ask the family a question and watch the votes come in: which weekend, which restaurant, which name for the dog.

For the holidays, start a gift list. Everyone adds wishes with a link or a photo, and Famlin can run a secret draw (with exclusions, so partners don't get each other). You only ever see who you are buying for.

How gift lists work →

Stories and family chat

Share a quick photo as a story that disappears after 24 hours, or pin it as a highlight to keep it around. Viewers can react or send you a private reply.

Each family can also get its own group chat for the quick back-and-forth that doesn't need to be a post. An admin turns it on per family.

How stories work → · Family chat →

And the rest

Circles

A smaller group inside a family, like just the grandparents, for posts that aren't for everyone. Members can always see who is in a circle.

Shared albums

One album the whole family adds photos to, for a wedding, a reunion or a summer at the cottage.

Your existing photo library

Link albums from Immich or a folder on your server, and filter them by person once an admin has matched faces to family members.

Share from any app

On your phone, tap Share → Famlin in Photos, Google Photos or WhatsApp and the photo lands in a new post.

A real web app

Everything in the browser on a laptop, with keyboard shortcuts. No install needed, so you can send a link to anyone.

Photos, done properly

HEIC from iPhones works everywhere, big photos are resized for viewing, and the original is kept on your server.

Notifications you control

Push and email, with a switch per type: new posts, comments, reactions, chat, stories, gift lists.

Your own copy

Any member can download everything they can see as a zip: posts, comments and the photos that go with them.

Five languages

The apps, the web app and the admin panel come in English, Dutch, French, European Portuguese and Simplified Chinese. Each person picks their own.

For whoever runs the server

Running Famlin for your family should take an evening to set up and a few minutes a month after that. Everything is managed from a web admin panel; you don't need to edit config files after the first start.

Look around the admin panel in the demo →

  • Sign-in your way. Email and password, Sign in with Apple, or any OpenID Connect provider: Google, Microsoft, Authentik, Keycloak and others.
  • Families and invites. Create families, send invite links, decide which post types each family can use.
  • Your name and colours. Give the app your family's name, logo and colour. The web app and the phone apps pick it up.
  • Scheduled backups. Daily or weekly database dumps plus a mirror of all photos, with an email if a backup fails or the disk runs low.
  • Storage where you want it. A Docker volume, a NAS share, or any S3-compatible bucket.
  • Move or restore in one go. Export everything as one zip, and restore it on a fresh server.
  • Moderation. Edit or remove posts and comments, and resend a notification that didn't arrive.

Install

Famlin is one Docker container plus Postgres. It runs on amd64 and arm64, so a cheap VPS, a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 or a Synology NAS all work.

  1. Run the installer on a fresh Debian or Ubuntu server (a few euros a month is plenty). It sets up Docker, HTTPS and automatic updates.
  2. Open /admin and create the first admin account. Then pick how people sign in.
  3. Invite your family. They install the app and enter your server's address, or just open it in a browser.
# on a fresh Debian/Ubuntu server $ curl -fsSL https://famlin.app/install.sh | sh # prefer to read it first? $ curl -fsSLO https://famlin.app/install.sh $ less install.sh && sh install.sh

Get the app

The apps are free. On first launch they ask for your server's address, so the same download works for every family. Prefer the browser? Every Famlin server also serves the full web app at its own address.

Want to try an app first? Enter demo.famlin.app as the server address and sign in with test@example.com / test123456.
